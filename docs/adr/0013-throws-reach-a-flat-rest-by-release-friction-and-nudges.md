# Throws reach a flat rest through the release, die-on-die friction and settle nudges, with a visible Cocked Rethrow as the fallback

The tray prototype showed that the 20-Dice cap rarely came to rest flat. In ADR-0010's 300 × 200 mm footprint, a Throw took about 5 attempts (7.4 in the leather hexagon), and 1 Throw in 6 in the hexagon had a Cocked Die after 12 attempts. The [flat-rest prototype](https://github.com/evildarkarchon/Andys-Dice/tree/prototype/flat-rest/prototypes/flat-rest) for [How does a 20-Dice Throw reach a flat rest?](https://github.com/evildarkarchon/Andys-Dice/issues/21) measured why. About 97% of Cocked Dice were propped on another Die, and most were tilted more than 10°, so the problem is Dice piling up, not a tolerance that is too strict. We fix it at the source: a one-layer, gentler release; lower die-on-die friction; and a deterministic nudge that tips a Die settling cocked onto the face it is nearest. Together, at a 4° tolerance, these bring the cap to 1.3–1.7 attempts per Throw on all three built-in Trays, with no Throw running out in 900. Whole-Throw retries stay silent and capped at 24. In the unreachable-in-practice case where all 24 fail, the Throw does not error. It plays the best attempt and then visibly throws only its Cocked Dice again, a **Cocked Rethrow**, as a player at the table would. The Roll Result still matches the Dice (ADR-0001), and a Roll still never ends with a Cocked Die.

## Decisions

- **Tolerance.** A Die is a Cocked Die when its best face normal (for the d4, the normal of its face most nearly down, negated) lies more than **4°** from vertical. This fills in ADR-0003's tolerance.
- **Release.** The Dice start in **one layer**, so no Die starts above another: up to 7 across and 3 deep at 30 mm, centred low over the near end of the Tray, and pulled in wherever a row would overhang the outline. They are thrown at about **0.65×** the tray prototype's speed, more gently downwards, with spin up to ±18 rad/s. All of this is drawn from ADR-0003's release generator. The exact numbers are tunables, not contracts.
- **Friction.** Die-on-die contacts combine to a friction of **0.2**, and die-on-floor and die-on-wall contacts keep the values each Tray's surface and wall material give them (ADR-0010). How the combination is done (per-pair combine callback or rescaled materials) is left to the build.
- **Settle nudge.** During a pre-simulation, a Die that has been nearly still (under about 3 mm/s and 0.4 rad/s) for 0.15 s while tilted past the tolerance gets a spin about the axis that would level its reading face (5 rad/s) and a 4 cm/s hop. It then tips onto its nearest face before Jolt puts it to sleep (0.35 s). A Die gets at most **3 nudges** per attempt. The nudge draws nothing from any generator, so it never shifts a result or a later release. Because it happens inside the attempt, the recording stays one continuous motion, and its contacts make sound like any other (ADR-0012).
- **Silent retries.** A Throw (a first Throw or a Reroll Throw) retries silently, as a whole, up to **24 attempts**, exactly as ADR-0001 and ADR-0006 describe. This fills in ADR-0001's retry cap. With the settings above, the estimated chance of exhausting it is below 1 in 10⁹ on every built-in Tray, and the worst case costs about 0.11 s of pre-simulation (about 4.5 ms an attempt on the driving dev's machine).
- **Cocked Rethrow, the fallback.** If all 24 attempts fail, the Throw accepts the attempt that came to rest with the fewest Cocked Dice. Its flat Dice stay frozen where they lie, as in a Reroll Throw (ADR-0006). Its Cocked Dice are picked up the way a rerolled Die is (ADR-0010: a glow, then a lift) and thrown again, with their own 24 silent attempts. A Throw may need at most **3** Cocked Rethrows. Past that, or if no attempt came to rest at all (every attempt escaped or timed out), the Roll fails as ADR-0001's error, never a fudged result. In the prototype's runs, rethrowing only the Cocked Dice always converged, within 3 rounds at these settings.
- **A Cocked Rethrow is not a Reroll.** It replaces no Face Value: the Cocked Die never had one. The Face Value chosen for that Die (ADR-0001) is applied by the numbering rotation for the Cocked Rethrow's final pose. It adds no step to the Die's chain of Face Values, no entry to the breakdown, nothing to the Roll History (ADR-0007), and nothing to `rr`'s 100-round backstop. It is pre-simulated with the rest of the chain before playback (ADR-0006), so its error semantics are unchanged.
- **Escapes never reach the player.** An Escaped Die or a timed-out attempt is always re-simulated silently. Only a Cocked Die can lead to a Cocked Rethrow.

## Considered Options

- **A wider tolerance.** Rejected. Only about 20% of Cocked Dice are within 10° of flat. 8° buys about 10% fewer attempts, while 15° counts visibly propped Dice as flat and nears the d20's 21° point, where its up face becomes ambiguous.
- **Rethrowing only the Cocked Dice as the default.** Rejected as the default. It always converges, but even at these settings 24–41% of 20-Dice Throws would show the player a pickup and rethrow. It is kept as the fallback.
- **More attempts alone.** Rejected. With the tray prototype's release, the hexagon still fails about 1 Throw in 1,000 at 48 attempts.
- **Stricter sleep thresholds.** Rejected. A 0.2 cm/s threshold over 0.6 s changed nothing and made each attempt 30% slower: leaning Dice are stably propped, not frozen mid-fall.
- **An error when the cap is exceeded (ADR-0001 as written).** Kept only as the backstop behind the Cocked Rethrow.

## Consequences

- **Amends ADR-0001.** A Throw that exceeds the retry cap is followed by a Cocked Rethrow instead of an error. The error remains only past 3 Cocked Rethrows, or when no attempt came to rest.
- **Amends ADR-0003.** The cocked tolerance is 4°, and the release is the one-layer, gentler one. The nudge is part of the engine-neutral simulator's contract: a fake simulator need not nudge, but the Jolt one must.
- **Amends ADR-0006.** A Throw in the chain that exceeds the cap gets Cocked Rethrows before the Roll Expression can fail.
- **Testing (ADR-0005).**
  - *The fake simulator* returns cocked attempts 24 times. Tests check that a Cocked Rethrow of exactly the Cocked Dice follows, that it adds no step to any chain, and that a fixed seed gives the same Face Value sequence. A fourth failing Cocked Rethrow is an error.
  - *Real Jolt scenario tests* check that a nudged Die ends flat and that its recording has no discontinuity.
  - *The `slow` soak test* throws the 20-Dice cap into each built-in Tray and fails if the mean attempts per Throw exceed 2.5, or if any Throw needs a Cocked Rethrow, in its run. These thresholds are tunables.
- **Release checklist (ADR-0005).** The gentler release is checked by eye (it must still read as a throw, not a drop), and so is the nudge (a settling Die tipping onto its face must look natural). The prototype measured both, but they have not yet been judged in motion.
- **Dice cap headroom.** With these settings, 25 Dice in the default Tray take 2.1 attempts per Throw (against 20.8 before), which the performance targets can use when they fix the cap.
